# C2B Logo — Origins

**Status:** Historical record. These are **earlier identity iterations**, not current
brand assets. Preserved 24 July 2026 from
`OneDrive/Pictures/Camera Roll/c2bLogo/` so all C2B imagery lives in one
version-controlled place.

**The current mark is `../c2b-logo-gold-hero.svg`** (vector master) and its rasters in
the parent folder. Nothing here supersedes those.

## Why this set matters

Two things are visible here that are not obvious from the current assets alone:

1. **The brand began teal/blue and flat**, then moved to gold and dimensional. The
   current *"deep charcoal / near-black backgrounds, gold as premium signature colour,
   silver/platinum accents, possible subtle blue or teal undertones"* direction is not
   arbitrary — the blue/teal was the *original* identity, and it survives as an accent
   family. That gives the gold ⇄ blue-platinum hero transition
   (see `../hero-logo-transition-concept.md` and `../transition/`) a genuine basis in
   the brand's own history rather than being a late aesthetic choice.

2. **The wordmark has been progressively dropped.** Early lockups carry
   `c2bwebdesign` plus a tagline; the current hero asset is the bare mark. That
   simplification is a real decision worth remembering before anyone reintroduces a
   tagline.

## Caveats

- **Raster only.** No vector source here — the vector master is `../c2b-logo-gold-hero.svg`.
- **Geometry drifts between iterations.** The `2` and the `b` bowl differ across these
  files; they are not all the same curve. Do not treat any of these as a geometry
  source.
- Only `c2b-07-...-transparent.png` has a real alpha channel.
- Filenames were normalised on import (originals: `c2b1/2/3.png`, `c2b_Logo.png`,
  `C2B_Logo-image.png`, `C2B-LogoTranspatent.png`, `Logo=Morph.png`). Contents verified
  byte-for-byte identical to source (`cmp`, 7/7); nothing re-encoded or resized.
