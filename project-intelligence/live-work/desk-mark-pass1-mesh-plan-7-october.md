## Step 3 — The geometry: two solids, joined, rounded, capped (`logo-mark-geometry.ts`)

The mesh topology does not change, and the footprint stays the trace. **Only the height function changes, in this
fixed order: join → round → cap [A3].**

1. **The two solids, WITHOUT the lip [A1].** `T(x, y)` is the round profile of `d_t` (the distance to
   `LOGO_TUBE_OUTLINE`, with the cut excluded), continued downward below the foot rather than flattening into the lip.
   `B(x, y)` is the stem's profile from the box's inside distance `e`: a straight bevel up to a FLAT face at the
   strokes' crest height, with its line continued downward below the foot. The box's own distance makes the hip-roof
   ends.
2. **Join:** `U = max(T, B)`. Where the bowl's crest runs onto the stem's face, both are flat and level, so the max is
   smooth there. Where the bowl's flanks cross the stem's bevel, the max leaves two concave creases.
3. **Round [A1, A2]:** a rolling-ball closing of `U` with radius `filletR`, on a fine grid inside
   `LOGO_JUNCTION_WINDOW`. Only the **fill** is kept: `F = max(0, closing(U) − U)`. It is interpolated bicubically and
   **clamped at ≥ 0**, then ADDED to the exact `U`. Wherever the closing changed nothing, the surface is exact, so the
   stem's face cannot gain a bump. **No blend ramp. The check is `F = 0` along the whole window border, the lip area
   included.**
4. **The lip back on:** `S = max(U + F, wallH)` inside the outline; `wallH` for `d < lipW` (as now).
5. **Cap [A3], last:** `H = min(S, E)`, where `E` is **the higher of the round and the straight-bevel profiles** of the
   FULL traced outline's distance. The cap therefore engages only where the trace and the joined solids disagree: the
   two inside corners and the stem's four outer corners. ⚠ **A min leaves a ridge, and the rounding does not smooth
   it.** That ridge's sharpness is measured (below) and shown to Carl. It is not hidden.

**Normals — one method over the whole surface [A4]:** central differences of the final `H` at a fixed step `h`
(stated in the module; ≤ ¼ `filletR`, and well above float noise), for interior, clip-loop AND band vertices. This
replaces the band's chain rule through `d`, R and c, which no longer describes `H`. The wall, lip and back keep their
exact flat normals. The `flat` mode remains the check on all of it.

**Removed:** `stemBlend`, `stemCrown`, the ease, and `maxNormalAngleStemEaseDeg`. Its value is already in the baseline.
⚠ **Removing the crown undoes Carl's 3 October request** (*"part of thr b is the wrong way round. showing the flat
back"* — a dead-flat face under the studio light read as the back seen through a hole). **This is said at the
checkpoint and carried to pass 2 as a question for the material and lights. It is not quietly fixed by putting the
crown back** [should-fix].
**Dials:** `stemR` (the bevel width) and `filletR`, which starts at the traced inside-corner radius.

**New readouts:**
- the junction window's max normal angle and max dihedral angle, against the step 1 baseline;
- the max `F` along the window border (must be 0);
- the cells where `F > 0`;
- **lip height:** the max |z − wallH| over every outer-ring point (must be 0) [A3a];
- **cap-active count**, per region: inside corners, stem corners, elsewhere — and "elsewhere" must be 0 [A3b];
- **cap ridge sharpness:** the max dihedral angle across the cap's crossing at each corner [A3];
- build ms against chunk 1's ~0.8 s; the closing costs about cells × (filletR ÷ step)² [should-fix].
