## 2\. Core Project Identity

C2B Web Design is a premium web design agency being built by Carl Buckley.

The website itself is the first portfolio piece.

There are currently no legacy client projects being relied on as proof. The site must demonstrate quality through its own execution:

* premium visual design
* careful typography
* motion quality
* interaction quality
* guided enquiry design
* business clarity
* future-facing but human-led technology

Target clients are businesses willing to invest approximately £2,000–£5,000+ per project, plus possible ongoing retainers and future digital services.

The business should not feel like:

* a generic web design shop
* an AI gimmick site
* a tech-bro SaaS startup
* a cheap lead-generation funnel
* a developer showing technical tricks

It should feel like:

* a premium creative studio
* a business perception partner
* a careful technology guide
* a human-led AI adoption partner
* a studio that understands quality, trust, and commercial outcomes

## 4\. Wider Business Vision

C2B is not intended to remain only a website design business.

Website design is the entry point.

The website is the front door into deeper business relationships.

The intended ladder is:

1. Build premium websites.
2. Build trust and credibility.
3. Understand how client businesses actually operate.
4. Identify friction, missed opportunity, weak enquiry flow, admin drag, or poor digital presentation.
5. Introduce practical AI-assisted tools or workflows where they genuinely help.
6. Over time, become a trusted digital systems and AI-adoption partner for small and medium businesses.

Possible future areas include:

* enquiry systems
* lead capture and organisation
* client research
* outreach systems
* marketing workflows
* admin reduction
* internal tools
* sales consultation tools
* customer visualisation tools
* sector-specific AI-assisted tools
* workflow and business operations systems

The goal is not to sell AI for its own sake. The goal is to help businesses use technology carefully, profitably, and under human control.

## 5\. Human-First AI Ethos

A central belief behind C2B is that AI is powerful, but must be governed by humans.

AI should not be presented as magic, replacement, or gimmick.

AI should be presented as capability under human direction.

Important principles:

* AI is powerful; that is why it needs governance.
* Humans provide intent, judgement, ethics, taste, responsibility, and accountability.
* Automation must not damage trust.
* Businesses should not be pushed into AI systems they do not understand.
* Careless AI implementation can damage confidence and make a business less likely to adopt useful technology in future.
* Trust matters more than hype.
* Practical value matters more than spectacle.
* AI adoption should be calm, understandable, and commercially useful.

A useful line:

> AI is capability. Humans provide intent, judgement, ethics, and accountability.

C2B should communicate this visually and verbally.

## 12\. Carl’s Working Preferences

When working with Carl:

* Explain before acting: say what you are about to do and why.
* Work in small stages.
* Complete and confirm one stage before moving to the next.
* Use plain English.
* Do not leave technical terms unexplained.
* Show a plan first for significant features or strategic changes.
* Do one thing at a time.
* Confirm before installing tools, packages, or dependencies.
* Check current stable versions before recommending technical packages, frameworks, libraries, or workflows.
* Teach principles, not just instructions.
* Preserve Carl’s learning.
* Avoid patronising explanations.
* Use music, DAW, production, Resolve, routing, mix, timing, phrasing, and arrangement analogies when helpful.
* Respect that Carl is a beginner in web development but not a beginner in creative or technical systems.

## 14a\. C2B Ethos: Theme, Restraint, and Physical Consequence

The C2B site is not merely an information source. It is the front door for a
business selling front doors. The experience itself must demonstrate the
standard C2B claims to offer.

The site should have a recurring theme with variations: a coherent physical
language of form, restraint, light, motion, material, rhythm, and consequence.
Cards, buttons, rails, logo, hero, text, and transitions should feel like they
belong to the same world rather than being separate effects.

Carl's creative references matter:

* Prefer David Gilmour restraint over Yngwie Malmsteen excess.
* Aim for the emotional discipline of "Comfortably Numb" more than visual
  showboating: space, tone, timing, release, and one or two moments that land.
* Motion should feel musical: phrased, legato, paced, and choreographed.
* Nothing should feel like a sudden UI toggle unless there is a deliberate
  reason. Elements should complete their phrase before the next phrase begins.
* Final tuning should be treated like mastering in music production: balance,
  tempo, brightness, contrast, emphasis, breathing room, and emotional flow.

Important visual rule:

> Effects should feel caused by the world, not layered on top of it.

Examples:

* A glowing filament should affect nearby card light, not sit as an isolated
  overlay.
* A logo emerging from video into code should affect nearby text and ambience.
* White/cool light should shift warmer near amber light sources and fade with
  distance.
* More selected glowing objects should mean more light in the shared
  environment.

This does not mean the site should be busy. The ambition is high, but the
execution should be controlled, premium, and incremental. Build the track before
adding automation. Prove one object, one motion phrase, or one light behaviour
before rolling it out.

## 18\. Motion \& Interaction Philosophy

Motion should:

* support clarity
* guide attention
* create elegance
* reinforce premium feel
* feel calm and legato
* support the active focus

Avoid:

* hyperactive animation
* constant movement
* excessive parallax
* demo-reel motion design
* mechanical stepper behaviour
* anything that pressures the user

The best motion often feels almost invisible.

Motion should make the process feel alive, not make the visitor feel observed, rushed, or entertained for no reason.

Useful principle:

> Mechanical feels like admin. Legato feels like guidance.

## 19\. Enquiry Experience Principles

The `/start` enquiry experience is not a standard contact form.

It is a guided process.

Key principles:

* Capture attention.
* Guide intention.
* Reveal certainty.
* The active question owns attention.
* Previous/contextual text supports the process but does not distract.
* Motion must be calm, bounded, atmospheric, and non-urgent.
* The user must never feel rushed.
* Selection should feel like activation, not administration.
* Cards should feel material, not like form controls.
* The system should help the user think.

Current approved direction:

* Stage 1 opening reveal is calm, legato, and mask-based.
* Begin creates conscious intention.
* Opening text remains as context rather than disappearing.
* Stage 2 begins with Q5 as the first visible question in a countdown-style sequence.
* Q5 is an orientation cue, not a permanent label.
* Multi-select is used where more than one answer may be true.
* “Next step” appears only after at least one answer is selected.
* The user stays in control.

## 20\. Client-Facing Copy Principles

Avoid technical jargon in client-facing copy.

Translate technique names into business benefit language.

Examples:

* Not “glassmorphism”
* Say “a frosted glass effect that gives the site a modern, premium feel”
* Not “GSAP scroll-triggered animation”
* Say “movement that guides attention as the visitor moves through the page”
* Not “React Three Fiber”
* Say “interactive 3D visuals where they genuinely support the story”

The site should talk like a trusted guide, not a developer résumé.

## 30\. Performance Philosophy

Performance is part of perceived luxury.

The project rejects:

> beautiful but sluggish

Targets and principles:

* strong Lighthouse scores
* stable Core Web Vitals
* minimal layout thrashing
* efficient GPU usage
* controlled rendering costs
* smooth mobile performance
* cinematic sophistication without visible strain

Performance quality and design quality are connected.
