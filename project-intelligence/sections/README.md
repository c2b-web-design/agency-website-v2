# Sections

**One file per section of the site.** The record of *why a section is the way it is* — its
lineage, Carl's reasoning, the ethos, and the design consistency running through it.

**Ruling: D-059** (23 August 2026). Read that entry for the reasoning; this file is the
working instruction.

---

## ⛔ EVERY SECTION IS OPEN. NOTHING HERE IS A CLOSURE.

⚠ **THE HERO IS THE LAST THING TO BE BUILT.** When it is done, **Carl walks the whole site
— a mastering session, in the DAW sense (D-035)** — and **sections are declared closed
THEN, together.**

⛔ **A section cannot be judged alone when it is meant to relate to the others.** That is
why closure is collective, and why it comes last.

⛔ **A FILE IN THIS FOLDER THAT LOOKS FINISHED IS NOT A CLOSURE.** Every section file says
on its face that its section is open. **D-046 is the worked failure** — it recorded a
completion while the enquiry corridor was open, and that corridor is open now.

⚠ **Built + approved by eye + recorded ≠ closed** (D-058). The three are different states
and this folder must not collapse them.

## What goes in a section file

### 1. A lineage line, at the top

**What the section descends from and what it inherits.** One or two sentences.

> *the client info section is a child of the Q+A section*

⚠ **This is the part that is safe to share early and useful early** (D-058). Lineage is
**stable**; implementation is not. ⛔ **It says what the section descends from and what it
inherits — and NOTHING about how the parent was built.**

### 2. Two voices

| Voice | Content | Who writes it |
|---|---|---|
| **The Architect's account** | What was decided, and why | The Architect |
| **Carl's account** | The ethos | ⛔ **Carl. Cannot be delegated.** |

⛔ **CARL'S ACCOUNT CANNOT BE DELEGATED, AND D-056 IS THE PROOF.** *"The answers have done
their job"* — the reason the card exit is deliberately asymmetric — **existed nowhere in
the record until he said it.** No amount of reading the code or the decisions would have
produced that sentence. ⚠ **An agent writing it would have written something plausible and
wrong.**

---

## ⛔ WHEN A SECTION FILE IS WRITTEN

**As the section NEARS COMPLETION. Not per chunk.**

⚠ Elements get modified as a section expands. A per-chunk record would rewrite itself
continuously while describing something still moving — and would be wrong more often than
right.

---

## ⚠ This folder is NOT `components/`

**`project-intelligence/components/` is an ARCHIVE** (D-057). Two files live there, both
from the project's first month; **no new component docs are written.** The component-doc
requirement is retired.

**This folder is different in kind.** A component doc described *a component*. A section
file records **why a section is as it is** — reasoning and ethos, not implementation.
